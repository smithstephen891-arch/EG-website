The mission of Elizabeth's Gift is to bless individuals and families living with special needs by helping provide mobility equipment — from wheelchairs and adaptive devices to accessible vehicles and lifts. Anything that helps someone experience greater freedom and quality of life.

 

You're Invited to the
Townies Benefit Dinner

This year's Townies party night will be an evening to celebrate the mission of Elizabeth's Gift, and we'd love to have you join us! Come enjoy an all-you-can-eat dinner and drinks, and get ready to bid on some exciting silent auction items. Most importantly, you'll get to hear more about Elizabeth's Gift, the people we serve, and the work we are doing together.

This event is open to anyone who wants to join us. Whether you have been a part of this journey or are just hearing about us for the first time, we would love to have you at the table.

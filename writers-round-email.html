We Hope You'll Join Us

Tomorrow night, Parker Welling, Casey Beathard, and Jonathan Smith are playing a writers' round at The Listening Room Cafe. If you've never been to one, it's a small, in-the-round setup where the songwriters take turns playing and telling the stories behind their songs. It's one of our favorite ways to spend an evening in Nashville.

And here's the part we love: a portion of every ticket comes back to Elizabeth's Gift. That money goes straight toward the mobility equipment we help provide for families living with special needs. So you get a great night of music, and a family gets a little closer to the equipment they need. We'd love to see you there.
